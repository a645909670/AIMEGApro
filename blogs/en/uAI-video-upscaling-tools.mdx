# Best AI Video Upscaling Tools in 2025

As video content quality becomes more important, AI video upscaling tools are helping creators enhance resolution, clarity, and overall visual quality. These tools use advanced AI algorithms to upscale low-resolution footage into high-definition videos effortlessly. Platforms like [Topaz Video AI](https://www.topazlabs.com/video-enhance-ai), [Runway](https://runwayml.com/), and [Pixop](https://www.pixop.com/) are leading the market.

## Why Use AI Video Upscaling Tools

AI video upscaling tools provide several advantages:  
- **Enhance video quality:** Turn low-resolution footage into high-definition output.  
- **Time-saving:** Automate the upscaling process without manual editing.  
- **Preserve details:** Improve sharpness and reduce artifacts using AI.  
- **Cost-effective:** Avoid reshooting footage while improving quality.

## Top AI Video Upscaling Tools

### [Topaz Video AI – Advanced Video Upscaling](https://www.topazlabs.com/video-enhance-ai)
Topaz Video AI uses deep learning to upscale videos, remove noise, and enhance details, making it ideal for professional content creators.  

**Pros:** High-quality results, AI-powered enhancements, supports multiple formats  
**Cons:** Requires a powerful computer, paid software  

### [Runway – AI Video Enhancements](https://runwayml.com/)
Runway offers AI video upscaling along with other editing tools, allowing creators to enhance video resolution and clarity with ease.  

**Pros:** Multiple AI features, cloud-based options, user-friendly interface  
**Cons:** Some features require subscription  

### [Pixop – Cloud-Based Video Upscaling](https://www.pixop.com/)
Pixop provides cloud-based AI video upscaling, enabling users to process large volumes of footage without local hardware limitations.  

**Pros:** Cloud processing, batch upscaling, customizable AI models  
**Cons:** Paid plans required for large-scale processing  

### [DVDFab Enlarger AI – Video Resolution Enhancement](https://www.dvdfab.cn/enlarger-ai.htm)
DVDFab Enlarger AI enhances video resolution from 480p to 1080p or 4K using AI algorithms, suitable for old footage restoration.  

**Pros:** Simple workflow, high-quality upscaling, AI noise reduction  
**Cons:** Limited to Windows, subscription required  

## Tips for Using AI Video Upscaling Tools

- Test different AI models to find the **best upscaling results** for your footage.  
- Use **clean source footage** to maximize AI enhancement quality.  
- Combine upscaling with **color correction and noise reduction** for optimal results.  
- Optimize output format and resolution based on **platform requirements**.  

## How AI Video Upscaling Improves Content Production

By leveraging AI video upscaling tools, creators can:  
- Restore old or low-resolution footage to modern standards  
- Produce higher-quality videos without expensive reshoots  
- Enhance audience viewing experience across platforms  
- Save time and effort in post-production  

AI video upscaling tools are revolutionizing video quality enhancement in 2025, making professional-grade visuals accessible to both creators and businesses.
