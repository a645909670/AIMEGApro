## Avatar Quality

- **Synthesia**: Known for polished, business-ready avatars. Great for training, onboarding, and formal videos.
- **HeyGen**: Slightly more expressive avatars and wider style variety (including social-media-ready avatars).

**Verdict**:  
Use **Synthesia** for corporate and education.  
Choose **HeyGen** for storytelling, YouTube shorts, and creative content.

## Voice Generation

- **Synthesia**: Offers professional-grade TTS voices. More formal and steady in tone.
- **HeyGen**: More energetic and modern-sounding voices. You can even upload your own for cloning.

**Verdict**:  
**HeyGen** has the edge for **voice variety** and cloning flexibility.
