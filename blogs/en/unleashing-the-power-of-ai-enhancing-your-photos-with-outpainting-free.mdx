## 2. Canva AI: Good for Layout, Not Outpainting

Canva has introduced AI features like Magic Resize and AI-generated visuals. It’s excellent for layout design, branding, and marketing material.

However:
- It’s **not optimized for true image expansion**  
- It focuses on templates, not canvas-aware AI  
- Image editing capabilities are still human-guided

Use Canva if you’re working on presentations, ads, or branded visuals—but combine it with AIMEGApro for smart canvas growth.

## 3. Photoshop Generative Fill: Powerful, But Not for Everyone

Adobe Photoshop now includes a **Generative Fill** tool powered by Firefly AI. It’s flexible and powerful, but has some drawbacks:

- Requires an Adobe Creative Cloud subscription  
- Has a steep learning curve  
- Better suited for professionals with experience  

If you already use Photoshop and know how to mask layers, Generative Fill is a great tool. But for the rest of us, it’s **overkill** for simple visual expansion tasks.

## 4. Runway ML: Visual AI for Editors and Creators

Runway is an impressive AI suite for creatives. It supports:

- Video background removal  
- AI training models  
- Image editing tools  
- Inpainting and outpainting

That said, it’s best for video professionals and artists working on advanced visuals. Most tools are **behind a paywall**, and there’s a learning curve.

For pure image expansion in a few clicks, **AIMEGApro is faster and easier**.

## 5. DALL·E 3: Amazing Generation, Limited Expansion

DALL·E’s prompt-to-image system is one of the most creative AI engines out there. However:

- It’s built for **image creation**, not editing  
- Expansion (outpainting) is often prompt-dependent  
- Free access is limited  
- Results may require refinement  

DALL·E is excellent for generating a concept or artwork—but **not ideal for taking an existing image and cleanly extending it**.

## 6. Remove.bg and Fotor: Utilities, Not Creation Suites

These platforms are great for specific tasks:

But they don’t offer contextual expansion or AI-driven outpainting. You’ll need another tool (like AIMEGApro) if your goal is to intelligently expand your canvas and match lighting, colors, or style.
