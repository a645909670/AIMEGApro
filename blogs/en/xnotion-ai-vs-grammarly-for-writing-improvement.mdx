## Writing Quality & Accuracy

- **Grammarly**: Excels at catching grammar, punctuation, tone, and sentence structure errors. Offers suggestions in real-time.
- **Notion AI**: Good for generating first drafts, rewriting, summarizing, but less accurate in grammar correction.

**Verdict**:  
If your goal is **perfect final copy**, Grammarly is more reliable.

## Productivity & Workflow

- **Notion AI**: Useful for brainstorming, outlining, and expanding thoughts. Integrated with Notion docs for seamless productivity.
- **Grammarly**: More suitable for final polishing and email writing, with integrations across Gmail, Word, Chrome, and Slack.

**Verdict**:  
- Early-stage content? Use **Notion AI**.  
- Final-stage edits? Use **Grammarly**.
