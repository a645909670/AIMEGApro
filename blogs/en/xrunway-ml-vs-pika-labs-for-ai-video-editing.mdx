## Video Generation & Animation

- **Runway ML**: Offers **text-to-video**, **image-to-video**, and **video-to-video** tools. Supports background removal, rotoscoping, and style transfer.
- **Pika Labs**: Focused on **text-to-video** using detailed prompts. Recently added camera motion & frame control.

**Verdict**:  
- Need complex video edits or pro workflows? Go with **Runway ML**.  
- Want fast results from prompts? Try **Pika Labs**.

## Output Quality & Creative Control

- **Runway ML**: High-resolution output, customizable aspect ratios, and timeline edits.
- **Pika Labs**: Fast rendering but with some resolution limitations. More style presets but less editing after generation.

**Verdict**:  
- **Runway ML** gives you more **post-generation control**.  
- **Pika Labs** is better for **quick, visually stunning demos**.
