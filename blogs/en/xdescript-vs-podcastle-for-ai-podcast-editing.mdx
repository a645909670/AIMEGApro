## Text-Based Editing

- **Descript** pioneered text-based podcast editing—you delete words in the transcript, and the audio follows.
- **Podcastle** offers similar functionality with a slightly more beginner-friendly interface.

**Verdict**:  
Both are great—but **Descript** feels more polished and flexible for long episodes.

## AI Voice Cloning

- **Descript Overdub**: Clone your voice and fix mistakes without re-recording.
- **Podcastle Revoice**: Also clones voice, but with more natural prosody and a smoother interface.

**Verdict**:  
**Podcastle’s Revoice** sounds more human, but Descript offers more customization.

---

## Recording & Collaboration

- Both support **remote interviews**, **multitrack sessions**, and **live recording** with built-in editors.
- Descript adds **screen recording**, which is perfect for podcasters also publishing on YouTube.

**Verdict**:  
If you want **video podcasting**, go with **Descript**.  
For **pure audio workflows**, **Podcastle** is faster.

## Publishing & Hosting

- **Descript**: Can publish to YouTube, export to .mp3, .mp4, or share links.
- **Podcastle**: Offers **built-in podcast hosting** with RSS feed management.

**Verdict**:  
If you want an **all-in-one hosting + editing tool**, **Podcastle** wins.
