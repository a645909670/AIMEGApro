## How Other Free AI Tools Compare

Here’s what you can expect from the most common alternatives:

**DALL·E (by OpenAI)**  
While powerful, DALL·E is not truly free. Its API model requires credits, and free access is limited. Outputs are also watermarked unless you're a paid user. Plus, using it well requires careful prompt engineering—something casual users may struggle with.

**Runway ML**  
This tool has a slick interface and strong video/image AI features, but its free plan is very limited. It usually includes watermarks and restricts resolution or project count.

**ClipDrop (by Stability AI)**  
Great for quick background fills and simple image tweaks. However, it lacks deeper control for canvas expansion or maintaining image style. It’s more of a utility than a creative workspace.

**PhotoRoom**  
Popular among ecommerce sellers, PhotoRoom offers background expansion and cleanup. The free version works well but reserves high-res and watermark-free exports for paid tiers.
