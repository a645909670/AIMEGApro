## Translation Quality

- **DeepL**: Provides **more natural-sounding** results, especially for European languages. Better with business context, idioms, and tone.
- **Google Translate**: Fast and broad but may sound robotic or too literal.

**Example**:  
Translating "We’d love to partner with you on this campaign"  
- DeepL: “Nous aimerions collaborer avec vous sur cette campagne.”  
- Google: “Nous aimerions nous associer à vous pour cette campagne.”  
DeepL’s version feels more **human and professional**.

## Integrations & APIs

- **DeepL**: Offers an API for paid users, ideal for translation plugins, eCommerce platforms, and document processing.
- **Google Translate**: Google Cloud API is widely integrated across tools, mobile apps, and third-party platforms.

**Verdict**:  
- Use **Google** for broad integration and scale.  
- Use **DeepL** for **high-accuracy B2B translation**.
