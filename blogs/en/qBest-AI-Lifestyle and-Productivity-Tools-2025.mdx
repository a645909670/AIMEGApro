## 3. Fitbod
🔗 [Fitbod](https://fitbod.me/)  
Fitbod uses AI to design **adaptive workout routines**, adjusting exercises based on progress and goals.

**Best for:** Home and gym users.

## 4. Studyable
🔗 [Studyable](https://studyable.com/)  
An AI study companion that helps students with flashcards, practice tests, and smart learning recommendations.

**Best for:** Students & lifelong learners.

---

## 5. Smodin
🔗 [Smodin](https://smodin.io/)  
Smodin is an **AI writing and research assistant** that helps with essays, citations, and translations.

**Best for:** Researchers & students.

---

## 6. WolframAlpha
🔗 [WolframAlpha](https://www.wolframalpha.com/)  
A computational knowledge engine that answers complex math, science, and data queries with precision.

**Best for:** Academics & professionals.

---

## 7. Gift Ideas AI
🔗 [Gift Ideas AI](https://giftideas.ai/)  
Generates **personalized gift recommendations** based on interests, events, and budget.

**Best for:** Shoppers & event planners.

---

## 8. Giftpack & DreamGift
🔗 [Giftpack](https://giftpack.ai/) | [DreamGift](https://dreamgift.ai/)  
AI-powered gift discovery platforms that simplify the process of finding the perfect present.

**Best for:** Corporate gifting & personal occasions.

---

## 9. Tripplanner & iPlan.ai
🔗 [Tripplanner](https://tripplanner.ai/) | [iPlan.ai](https://iplan.ai/)  
These tools design **custom travel itineraries** based on user preferences, making trip planning effortless.

**Best for:** Travelers.

---

## 10. Woebot Health
🔗 [Woebot Health](https://woebothealth.com/)  
An AI mental health chatbot providing support through CBT-based conversations.

**Best for:** Mental health & well-being.
