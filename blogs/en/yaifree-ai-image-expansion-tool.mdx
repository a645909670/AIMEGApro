## What Is AI-Powered Outpainting?

AI outpainting refers to expanding the boundaries of an existing image by using AI to intelligently generate new surrounding content. It fills in:

- Backgrounds  
- Missing elements  
- Sky, scenery, walls, or space around a subject  

The best part? It understands context—so lighting, colors, and shadows match the original image seamlessly.
