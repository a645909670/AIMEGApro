## What Does an Ecommerce-Focused AI Translator Do?

Unlike generic translators, ecommerce AI tools are trained to:

- 🛍️ Preserve brand voice and product tone

- 💰 Adapt price formats, sizing, and shipping info

- 🧠 Understand context—like materials, specs, and use cases

These tools are fast, accurate, and optimized for high-conversion content.
