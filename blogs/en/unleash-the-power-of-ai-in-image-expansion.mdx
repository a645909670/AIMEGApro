## What Is AI Image Expansion?

AI image expansion (also called **AI outpainting**) uses machine learning to analyze and intelligently "extend" your image. The result?

- Wider photos without stretching  
- Backgrounds that blend naturally  
- Extra space for text or cropping  
- Perfect compositions for web or print

It’s like having a designer in your browser—except it’s free, fast, and requires no training.
